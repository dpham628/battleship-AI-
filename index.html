    Game mode

    Deploy your fleet

    Pick a ship, then click your board. Press R or right-click to rotate. Click a placed ship to move it.

    The Healer ship

    Each side has a 2-cell Healer. On your turn you either fire or move the Healer one square or rotate it (it pivots on one of its cells). It can't pass through ships or hits, but it can move over the enemy's missed shots.

    Because of that, you may fire again at a cell you missed (click it twice to confirm) — the enemy Healer might be there now.

    If the Healer ends its move or rotation touching a burning (hit) cell of a ship that isn't sunk, it puts out that fire: the hit is repaired and the enemy can target it again. Repairs are announced.

    One hit disables the Healer for the rest of the game: it can't move, rotate, or repair anymore. A hit on a Healer looks like any other hit to the shooter; it's only named once it's sunk. Two hits sink it. Sink the five warships to win — a Healer left on its own doesn't keep a fleet alive.
