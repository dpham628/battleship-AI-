Your Fleet

    Deploy your fleet

    Pick a ship, then click your board. Press R or right-click to rotate. Click a placed ship to move it.

    The Healer ship

    Each side has a 2-cell Healer. On your turn you either fire or move the Healer one square (no rotation, only into cells the enemy hasn't fired at).

    If the Healer ends its move touching a burning (hit) cell of a ship that isn't sunk, it puts out that fire: the hit is repaired and the enemy can target it again. Repairs are announced.

    One hit immobilizes the Healer for the rest of the game; two hits sink it. Sink all six ships to win.
